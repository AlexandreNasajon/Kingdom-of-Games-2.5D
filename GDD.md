# Kingdom Of Games

## Aesthetics
2.5D Pixel art, Ancient Desert Kingdom

## Basic Mechanics
The game is divided into tiles and the player walks 1 tile horizontally or vertically each step. Similarly, objects must occupy tiles and npcs move according to them as well.
The player character and each npc occupies 1 tile.

The player can use wasd keys to move or d-pad in controller.
Press Spacebar/Start to open Menu.
Press Enter or E key/A to interact with characters and object or confirm options.
Press Esc or Q key/B to close a window or go back to a previous screen.

---

## Abstract
The world of Kingdom of Games revolves around tournaments. There is one main game: Cards&Coins, a TCG. There are 5 cities to explore. The way the player interacts with the world by winning tournaments, or trading and buying cards, directly affects the prices of cards and characters' reactions. The world is rich with emerging quests to acquire rare and powerful game pieces. The goal is to defeat the King of Games.

## Economy
- The minimum card price is 1.
- The maximum card price is 1000.

The player begins with 100 coins and can buy, sell or trade items. It is also possible to acquire items through exploration and interaction with npcs.

The market is the set of all cards available for trade. That includes cards owned by NPCs and cards owned by the player.

Opening packs of a card game lowers by 1% the price of every card in the market. It is simply supply and demand. The minimum a card can cost is 1.

Trading items with npcs means that npcs will either keep that item for you to trade again or he may tell you he sold that item to another npc and give you their location.

There is a list of every card and piece with their prices. Events triggered by the player can change those prices.

Convincing certain influencers to convince other npcs that a card is good. There are many influencer npcs that can influence the market, raising the price of certain cards by 50% to 500%!
If the player buys cards from npcs or shops, the price of those cards rises by 1%.
If the player is the only one with the card (absolute monopoly checking every npc in the game), the price rises 10 times!

### Normal prices of cards by rarity (assigned randomly by npcs):
- Common: 1–5 coins
- Rare: 10–100 coins
- Epic: 100–1,000 coins

There are many kinds of npcs that will give items ranging from random cards that npc says he found on the ground to a huge collection that an old npc spent his entire life building and decided to donate them to the player if the player listens to his philosophy lesson. There are certain npcs that the player can interact with and the npc will leave and appear in another city. The player must keep finding that npc until that npc decides to give the player epic cards and/or large collections or a box or something nice.

### How to make money:
- Winning tournaments
- Winning bets in games
- Buying low at a certain location and selling high at another
- Finding items on the ground and selling them
- Trading bad cards for good cards and then selling the good cards
- Interacting with npcs to get items and selling them

## Main Quest

The player must defeat the King of Games in Cards&Coins in order to become the next King of Games.

The player begins at the border of the kingdom, in a small Village. A young kid and his grandpa teach the player how to play Cards&Coins. They are the player's mentor figures. The grandpa instructs and the grandson challenges the player. The main goal of the first city is to acquire cards by talking to NPCs and learning about the world. The House of Glory will register the player as a new member and give them a starter deck and 1 pack.

With this, the player now has all the tools necessary to run rampant into the world of games, betting and manipulating the market.

There are tournaments in each city. Each tournament has 5 leagues, each with 3 rounds. To defeat the city champion, the player must first win the 5 leagues. After the player becomes champion in each city, he can go to the Capital to challenge the 4 king's guards and then the King of Games.

## Day and Night Cycle

Each part of the day is **6 minutes long** in real life time. Each day is **18 minutes** long.

|------|-------|

## Cards&Coins — Rules

For 2 players.

### Contents:
Each player must have:
- Deck of 30–60 cards
- Power tokens (+1 atk/def)
- Weakness tokens (−1 atk/def)

### Objective:
Reduce the opponent's life points to zero.
If a player would draw a card and has none left in the deck, they lose the game.

### Standard game:
No more than 3 copies of each card in a deck.

### Turn Structure:
1. **Collect:** Receive 2 coins and draw 1 card.
2. **Develop:** May play cards from hand.
3. **Activate:** Each allied unit may attack, defend, activate ability, or trigger another effect. Cards cannot be activated on the turn they enter play.
4. **End of turn.**

### Combat:
**Attacking:** Choose an enemy unit as target or attack the opponent directly. If there are defending units on the enemy field, they must be destroyed first.
- Compare attacker ATK with defender DEF:
  - If equal: both are destroyed.
  - If different: the lower is destroyed.
  - If DEF > ATK: attacker is destroyed; defender's DEF is reduced by attacker's ATK until end of turn.

**Defending:** Defending units protect the player and non-defending units. If the player has one or more defenders, they cannot be attacked directly and their non-defending units cannot be attacked.

**Ability:** Some units may have an ability. Activating the unit's ability counts as that unit's activation — the unit that activates its ability cannot attack or defend the same turn.

## Cards

### Cost types:
- **Yellow:** Pay Coin(s)
- **Blue:** Discard card(s)
- **Black:** Pay Life
- **Red:** Sacrifice unit(s)
- **Green:** Erase card(s) from graveyard

### Types:
- **Units** — stay on the field; can activate abilities, attack, or defend
- **Supports** — take effect and are discarded

### Card attributes:
- Type (unit, support)
- Text
- Rarity
- Units also have: Subtype, Attack, Defense

### Special cards:
- **Unique:** Only one copy allowed across all decks and reserves combined.

### Zones:
- **Deck** — draw cards from here
- **Graveyard** — destroyed and discarded cards
- **Oblivion** — erased cards
- **Field** — units controlled by each player

---

## Standard Effects

### Main activations:
- **Attack** — when this unit attacks [effect]
- **Defend** — when this unit becomes a defender [effect]
- **Ability** — [effect]

### Special activations:
- **Grudge** — if this card is in your graveyard [effect]

### Trigger effects:
- **Enter** — when this unit is deployed
- **Death** — if this unit is destroyed
- **Harvest** — triggers at the Collect Phase
- **Closure** — triggers at end of turn

### Passive effects:
- **Fly** — Can ignore defenders without fly. Cannot be attacked by enemies without fly, unless it is defending.
- **Overrun** — Excess combat damage this unit would deal to an enemy unit is instead dealt to the opponent.
- **Tenacity** — This unit isn't destroyed in a tie.
- **Dash** — This unit can be activated the turn it enters.

### Keywords:
- **Dig N** — send the top N cards of your deck to your graveyard
- **Erase target** — send target from field to oblivion zone
- **Destroy target** — send target from field to graveyard

Units may have more than one keyword for the same effect. E.g.: enter/attack — deal 2 damage.
